Privacy policy

This policy covers the Pocketpaws app for iPhone and iPad.

Pocketpaws does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app does not connect to the internet.

The app saves three things on your device so the game remembers you between sessions:

  • your progress, such as cleared stages, coins, chests and the animals you have unlocked;
  • your settings, such as sound, vibration and where you placed the touch controls;
  • which tutorial tips you have already seen.

This information stays on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and deleting the app deletes it.

Children. Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes. If a future version changes any of this, for example by adding ads, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
